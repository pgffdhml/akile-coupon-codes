## How to redeem without losing the 30-minute window

1. Sign in first. The coupon selector at checkout only appears for logged-in accounts.
2. Redeem the code in Finance Center → Coupons, using the "Redeem a code" field, or in the shop's Activity Redemption box with the Redeem Now button.
3. Check the Coupon List. Each coupon shows one of five states: Not Used, Used, Expired, Reserved by order, Revoked.
4. At checkout, pick the coupon in the selector under "Select Purchase Time". The price breakdown lists the original price, the coupon deduction and any configuration upgrade charge as separate lines.
5. If you are using `AK47`, finish the order inside 30 minutes of redeeming it.

Configuration upgrades are added after the deduction, so a coupon does not discount a RAM or disk upgrade. If a custom configuration is quoted through a ticket, do that before calculating what you save.

### Ongoing versus one-time, and the permanence of "Apply to server"

AkileCloud separates two usage modes in the Coupon List. An ongoing renewal discount can be attached to an existing server through "Apply to server": you pick the server, confirm, and the renewal price for that machine changes permanently while the coupon is consumed immediately. A one-time renewal discount is selected at renewal and reduces only that payment.

That distinction decides your year-two cost. `AkileCloudLsStart` is advertised as recurring, so it belongs in the ongoing bucket; most of the plan-specific codes do not say which mode they use, and the checkout page is where you find out.

## Billing rules that are not in the price table

Traffic over the monthly allowance is throttled to a shared 10 Mbps, not billed. A traffic reset costs ¥7.00 per time, and "Traffic Auto Renew" is a separate switch that pulls that fee from your balance automatically when the allowance runs out. Turn it off if you would rather be throttled than charged.

Automatic renewal runs three days before expiration and needs enough balance in the account. Once the paid period ends, the service agreement provides for automatic and irreversible deletion.

Payment options are account balance, Alipay, WeChat Pay, UnionPay, international cards and USDT on TRC20 or Polygon. Alipay and WeChat are QR flows, and several channels display a handling fee line that sits alongside the discounted service price. Currency conversion and bank charges are on the customer, and the amount has to arrive in full. Paid orders can be exported as a PDF invoice.

## Refunds, and who should not buy this

The refund window is 24 hours from provisioning, with no more than 5 GB of traffic used, and the money goes back to your account balance rather than the original payment method. Discounted plans and anything bought with a coupon are excluded from refunds entirely, which puts every purchase made with `AK47` or `AkileCloudLsStart` outside the window. Setup, installation and special configuration fees are non-refundable, and there is a cap of three machines per account per calendar month, one per node. The English-language agreement is stricter still and declines requests once service begins.

That combination matters more than the discount. If you are testing whether a node's routing suits your ISP, buy the cheapest plan in that family first, without a coupon, and check it inside the first day.

Skip AkileCloud for anything that needs an SLA, sustained high CPU, or large local disks. Entry plans ship with 5–10 GB of disk, and a LowEndTalk user running a $14/year Singapore box reported Geekbench 5 single-core around 1300 with visible CPU steal, which is fine for two Discord bots and a light API and not fine for a database. Some Asian nodes are not sold as mainland-direct, so read the routing note on the specific plan rather than the region.

What users do report consistently is the streaming unlock: Netflix, Disney+, YouTube, DAZN, Hulu and DMM work through the built-in DNS setup without extra configuration, Hong Kong 1 Gbps nodes have been measured above 900 Mbps, and most nodes sit above 99% availability. Documentation and support are Chinese-first.
